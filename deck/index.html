Q5 · POPIA

POPIA BINDS US IN FULL,
AND CAN REACH STRAVA TOO

MEMBER

Gives consent and can ask for a copy or deletion

THE CLUB

Responsible party: decides what is collected and why

MARU

Operator: processes for the club under a contract

HOSTING

Offshore cloud: cross-border safeguards apply

What it means for Maru

  • Register an information officer
  • Consent and a privacy notice for every club
  • Report breaches to the Regulator and to members
  • Opt-in before any marketing message
  • Penalties: fines up to R10 million; some offences carry prison

Do Strava and Spond have to comply?

POPIA covers foreign companies that use means in South Africa to process personal information, not just local ones. Whether a given app is caught is a legal question. Both publish GDPR-style policies.

For us it’s a selling point: compliance is built in, so the club’s committee carries less risk.

POPIA sections 3, 22 and 72, as summarised by DLA Piper and Bowmans. Not legal advice.
